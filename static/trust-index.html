About

How many people can contribute to a common goal before someone decides to ruin it all for their own amusement? That's what Circles of Trust seeks to answer. Each circle acts as a guestbook of people who've stopped by to grow its size. However, at any point, someone can choose to Betray the circle, dooming it and rendering it destroyed.

How long can your circle last? Each user can own one active circle at a time, when their circle is betrayed, the slot frees up. There are no limits to how many circles you can be in as a participant, so go ahead and join all the circles in sight... or betray them!

Your user name, circle name, and comments must only use alphanumeric symbols and spaces. User names and circle names must also be 20 characters or less in length.
