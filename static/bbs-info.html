About Asciiboard

This is a text-based bulletin board dedicated to the discussion of Toybox services and miscellaneous chit-chat about programming, life, the internet, and the universe. No sign-up is required - just choose your preferred name and jump right in.

The string of numbers next to a poster's name is a unique hash based on their IP address, so you know who's who, even if they don't provide a name.

Guidelines & Rules

All text submitted must match the regex: /^[a-zA-Z0-9!?.,\-_'"/()\s]+$/. This makes it easier to filter out low-effort drive-by spam. Beyond that:

  1. Don't be an ass.
  2. Don't bypass the restrictions put in place to prevent people from being asses.
  3. Don't post just for the sake of posting: make sure your posts have at least some value.
  4. If you're going to troll, at least be funny!

In addition to that, some guidelines enforced by the server:

  • Cannot create another thread if your thread is already at the top of the index.
  • Cannot create more than two consecutive posts in the same thread.
  • Cannot post more than once every 30 seconds.
