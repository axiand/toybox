About

This is a story written by internet users one word at a time. Everyone gets a turn at submitting the next word to the story. After a certain amount of words, a new chapter is created. You can view past chapters using the page list under the submit form.

Hover over words to see when they were submitted and who submitted them. The colors of the text underlines are unique based on the poster's IP address.

Note that it is not permitted to add more than one newline in a row (this does not consume your turn - if you see the submission form, you can submit!)
